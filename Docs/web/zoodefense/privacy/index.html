Ads and your choices

The game shows rewarded ads (only when you tap to watch one, for example for free gems) and occasional full-screen ads between matches. Players who have made any purchase do not see the between-match ads.

On first launch the game asks whether you accept personalised ads.

  • If you accept, our ad partners may use your advertising identifier and device data to show ads that match your interests. On iPhone you will then also see Apple's “Allow tracking” prompt, and you can still say no there.
  • If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. We tell our ad partners that you did not consent (GDPR) and that your data must not be sold or shared for cross-context behavioural advertising (CCPA / CPRA).

You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Personalised ads. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, which works with other ad networks. Each network handles data under its own privacy policy; the list of networks is available from Unity LevelPlay.

Purchases

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details. When you buy an item, the game sends the store's signed transaction to our server, which checks it with the store's certificate and records the transaction ID so an item is delivered exactly once and cannot be claimed by another player.

Online battles

When you play an online match, your player name, trophies, deck and in-match actions are sent to your opponent through Unity's relay servers. Your opponent does not receive your email, account or device details.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the game. They process data for us under their own privacy terms.

  • Unity Technologies: player accounts and sign-in, cloud backup, online match relay, purchase checking, and ad mediation (Unity LevelPlay). Unity privacy policy
  • Ad networks connected through Unity LevelPlay, for showing and measuring ads.
  • Apple and Google, for app distribution and payments.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers are covered by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Progress on your phone stays until you reset it or remove the app.
  • Your online account and cloud backup stay until you delete the account.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as needed to prevent fraud and meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Delete your data in the game

  • Settings › Account › Delete account deletes your online account and its cloud backup, and resets the game on your phone.
  • Settings › Support › Reset progress deletes the progress stored on your phone only.

Other requests

Email founder@nutrica.site with your Player ID (found in Settings › Support › Player ID). We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Zoo Defense is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent between the game and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to the game's server data is limited to the people who run the game.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For important changes we will also tell you in the game.
