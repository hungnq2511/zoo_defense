## Description
Defend your zoo in fast, head-to-head tower defense battles.

Pick five animals and four send-monsters, then race your opponent to survive wave after wave. Place your animals on the board, upgrade them mid-match, cast keeper spells and send monsters into your rival's path. The last zoo standing wins.

BATTLE REAL PLAYERS
• Ranked 1v1 matches against players of a similar trophy level
• Send-monsters straight into your opponent's waves
• Watch your opponent's board and react to their moves
• Quick emotes to greet, cheer or tease

BUILD YOUR DECK
• Collect animals with different elements, splash, slow and stun abilities
• Level up cards and unlock new ones from chests
• Save several deck presets and swap spells for every play style

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Adventure: 50 levels across 5 regions, each with its own twist
• Daily Challenge and Endless mode for high scores
• Weekend Mayhem Arena, Boss Raid and knockout Tournament
• Practice against a bot with no trophies at stake

PROGRESS THAT LASTS
• Season pass with free and premium rewards
• Link an account to back up your progress and move it to a new phone

Zoo Defense is free to play. Some items can be bought with real money; you can turn this off in your device's Screen Time settings. Drop rates for chests are shown in the shop.

## App Review information
- **Sign-in required:** No. The game starts as a guest; account linking is optional.
- **Notes for the reviewer:**

```
Zoo Defense is a 1v1 tower defense game. No login is needed: the game signs in anonymously on first launch.

How to test:
- Tap PLAY on the home screen for a ranked online match. If no other player is online, the game matches you with a practice bot after about 10 seconds, so a match always starts.
- Practice, Adventure and Daily Challenge are available from the play modes.
- In-app purchases are in the Shop (bottom bar). All products are consumables. "Restore purchases" is at the bottom of the Shop and re-delivers any purchase that was not finished.
- Chest drop rates are shown in the Shop ("Drop rates").
- On first launch the game asks for consent to personalised ads. The App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown only if the player accepts personalised ads.
- Account deletion: Settings > Account > Delete account (available after linking an account). Settings > Support > Reset progress clears local data.
- Privacy choice can be changed later in Settings > Privacy > Personalised ads.
```
